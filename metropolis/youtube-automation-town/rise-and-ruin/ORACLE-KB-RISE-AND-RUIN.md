## CONTENT SYSTEM
**Title formulas (hyper-specific + curiosity gap — a number always helps):**
- "How [Company] Makes Billions From [Unexpected Thing]" (e.g. "$1.50 hot dogs")
- "The Collapse of [Company]" / "Why [Company] REALLY Died"
- "How [Company] Quietly Controls [Market]"
- "The Rise and Fall of [Company]"
- "[Company]'s Genius Business Model, Explained"
- "The [Product / One Decision] That Destroyed [Company]"

**Thumbnail house style (niche-specific — NOT the war-channel style):**
- Recognizable company **LOGO or PRODUCT** front and centre — instant recognition is
  this niche's #1 CTR lever.
- Money/collapse visual language: **red DOWN arrow (ruin) / green UP arrow (rise)**,
  cash, or the product hero shot.
- 2–4 bold words, often a number or shock word: "GONE", "BILLIONS", "$1.50?", "WHY?"
- Emotion: shock / greed / downfall / secret. Reaction or CEO face optional.
- Consistent colour + frame system so the catalogue reads as one brand.

**Retention:** cold-open on the hook (the shocking number/outcome) BEFORE the backstory.
Re-hook every 60–90s ("but the real reason they collapsed was worse…").

**Long-form:** 20–40 min single-company deep dives. Compile themed multi-company
"dossiers" ("5 Retail Giants That Collapsed") for the watch-hour asset.

**Conversion stack:** subscribe watermark, end screens, pinned comments, newsletter link.

**Metadata:** business/finance tag base, keyword-rich description, chapters. Keep
framing **educational/story** — never "buy this stock" — to stay LOW-YMYL and safely
monetisable.

## LEARNINGS (ported + niche-specific)
- Packaging + retention win; the underlying content quality is table stakes.
- Hyper-specific hooks beat broad ones; **a number in the title/thumbnail lifts CTR.**
- **Recognizable brand/logo in the thumbnail** is the niche's biggest CTR lever.
- Educational/story framing = low-YMYL = safe monetisation (vs. risky advice framing).
- Long-form drives watch-hours — the only YPP gate left on this channel.
