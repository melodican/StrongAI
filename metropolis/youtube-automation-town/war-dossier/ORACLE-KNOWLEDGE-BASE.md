## CONTENT SYSTEM (proven)
- **Title formula:** "Why German [role] [feared / craved / refused / couldn't believe]
  American [specific thing]." Hyper-specific single-detail hooks beat broad ones.
- **Thumbnail house style:** one terrified/awed German soldier face (emotion MATCHES the
  video) + the American subject + a **red box, cream stencil, 2–4 power words** + one
  **white arrow**. Bright, high-contrast, phone-legible. HARD BAN: no SS runes,
  swastikas, or Nazi insignia (monetisation risk + illegal in Germany → region-block).
  Plain Wehrmacht helmets only.
- **Retention:** re-hook every 60–90s (forward-tease / withheld-answer / open question /
  mid-video reset / stakes-raise). Never touch a cold-open that already holds.
- **Long-form "dossiers" (the money format):** compile 4–6 related shorts into 35–50 min
  pieces. Strongest hook first; TRIM internal intros/outros (one master intro + one
  master outro); title cards between parts; YouTube chapters.
- **Conversion stack:** subscribe watermark (channel-wide), end screens on top videos,
  pinned comments, sub-confirmation links.
- **Metadata:** consistent tag base + keyword-rich description footer + educational
  disclaimer. Max 15 hashtags total or YouTube voids them all.

## LEARNINGS (data, not theory)
- Content was never the problem — **packaging and conversion were.** Retention runs
  **75–82%** (elite).
- Thumbnail overhaul lifted channel CTR **2.8% → 4.5%+**.
- ~**90% traffic is Suggested/Browse**, not search. Search is under-exploited.
- First breakout = the **Snipers / "what came next"** video (hit Browse/home feed).
- **Watch-hours accelerate via long-form** (~0.4–0.6 hrs banked per dossier view);
  **subscribers are the bottleneck gate.**
- **RPM realistic band: $8–12** (history is mid-tier; length + retention reach the top of
  the band, not finance/health territory). Not $20.
