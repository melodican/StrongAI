# The War Dossier — Subscribe CTA Lines
*Built to fix the conversion bottleneck: 462 views → only +2 subs (~0.4%).
Goal: lift sub conversion toward 1%+ by actually ASKING. Audience skews 65+ male
documentary viewers — so the tone is understated and value-framed, NOT
"smash that subscribe button." That register would feel cheap and lose this crowd.*

---

## The strategy: two CTAs, not one

Your end-screen CTA lands at ~15% retention — almost nobody's still there. So we add
a **soft mid-video CTA at a HIGH-retention moment** (right after a payoff beat, where
~45–55% are still watching), plus a **stronger end CTA**. On-screen, pair both with a
subscribe-button animation, and always run a subscribe + next-video end screen.

**Rule:** the mid-video CTA must come right after you've *delivered* something —
never before. You earn the ask, then make it. One short sentence. Don't linger.

---

## 1. MID-VIDEO SOFT CTA (place at ~40–50% mark, just after a payoff)

Pick one, rotate across videos so regulars don't hear the same line:

> "If files like this are your kind of history, The War Dossier opens a new one every
> week — the subscribe button keeps them coming to you."

> "There's a new dossier every week, and the next one's already being prepared. If
> you want it to find you, that's what subscribing does."

> "We dig into a story like this one every week. Subscribe and the next file comes
> straight to you — no need to go looking."

Keep it to a single breath. Then return immediately to the narration. Do not pause
the momentum you just built with the payoff.

---

## 2. END CTA (replaces the bare "Subscribe to The War Dossier" sign-off)

The current close just names the channel. Strengthen it with a *reason* + a
*forward hook* to the next video (which also feeds the end-screen click):

> "If this is the kind of history the books leave out, subscribe to The War Dossier —
> there's a new file every week, and the next one may be the most important yet.
> They're on screen now."

> "The War Dossier opens a new file every week — the stories the history books leave
> out. Subscribe so you don't miss the next one. And if you want another dossier
> right now, the next file is on screen."

Then your end screen shows: **Subscribe element + the single best next video** (point
viewers to a *proven* one — Pilots or Rations — not the newest upload).

---

## 3. ON-SCREEN (non-narration) — tell VidRush / your editor

- **Subscribe-button graphic** that animates in during BOTH CTA lines (a brief
  bottom-corner overlay; don't cover the footage).
- **End screen, last 15–20s:** Subscribe element + 1 video element pointing to a
  proven performer. Keep the narration light here so the end screen has room.
- Optional: a small persistent channel-handle watermark (@TheWarDossier) bottom-right
  the whole video — YouTube's built-in branding watermark doubles as a subscribe
  button on desktop.

---

## Why this should move the number
You're currently converting ~0.4% of views to subs. For the 1,000-sub monetisation
gate at that rate you'd need ~250,000 views. Just *asking* — most channels see
sub-rate jump materially the moment they add a real CTA — plausibly lifts you to
~1%+, which roughly **halves the views needed to hit 1,000 subs.** This is the single
cheapest lever on the channel: it works on traffic you're already getting.

> end sign-off for one of the strengthened versions above. Then it's automatic on
