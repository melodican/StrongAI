## THE CRITICAL EDIT (this is what separates a real dossier from a lazy glue-job)

**Goal: ONE intro at the very start, ONE outro at the very end, nothing repeated in between.**

- **Clip 1 (Artillery):** keep the cold-open INTACT (it's your master intro). **Trim only the OUTRO** — listen for *"Subscribe to The War Dossier — the stories the history books leave out"* at the end and cut from just before it. (~last 15–20s.)
- **Clips 2, 3, 4:** trim BOTH ends —
  - **Head:** cut the cold-open. Listen for the opening line up to *"…This is the dossier on…"* and cut everything before the first real content sentence after it. (~first 30–45s.)
  - **Tail:** cut the *"Subscribe to The War Dossier…"* outro. (~last 15–20s.)
- **Clip 5 (Combined Arms):** trim the HEAD (cold-open) like the others, but **KEEP the OUTRO** — this is your one master sign-off for the whole video.

> Why this matters: a full "…goodbye, subscribe" ending mid-video is a flashing EXIT
> sign. Removing the 4 internal outros is the single highest-impact edit for retention.
