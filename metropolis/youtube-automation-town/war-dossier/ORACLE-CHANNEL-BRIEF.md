## THE CONTENT SYSTEM (what works, proven this build)
- **Title formula:** "Why German [role] [feared / craved / refused / couldn't believe]
  American [specific thing]." Hyper-specific single-detail hooks outperform broad ones.
- **Thumbnail house style:** one terrified/awed German soldier face (emotion must MATCH
  the video's actual emotion) + the American subject + a **red box with cream stencil
  text (2–4 power words)** + one **white arrow**. Bright, high-contrast, readable at
  phone size. HARD BAN: no SS runes, swastikas, or Nazi insignia (monetisation + it's
  illegal to display in Germany → region-blocks). Plain Wehrmacht helmets only.
- **Retention:** insert a re-hook every 60–90s (forward-tease / withheld-answer / open
  question / mid-video reset / stakes-raise). Never touch the cold-open if it holds.
- **Long-form "dossiers":** compile 4–6 related short videos into 35–50 min pieces.
  Strongest hook first, TRIM internal intros/outros (keep one master intro + one master
  outro), title cards between parts, YouTube chapters. This is the money format.
- **Conversion stack:** subscribe watermark (channel-wide), end screens on top videos,
  pinned comments, sub-confirmation links. Emotion-honest thumbnails (don't over-promise).
- **Metadata:** consistent tag base + keyword-rich description footer + educational
  disclaimer. Max 15 hashtags total or YouTube ignores them all.

## WHAT WE'VE LEARNED (data, not theory)
- **Content was never the problem — packaging and conversion were.** Retention runs
  **75–82%** (elite). The whole early struggle was CTR and subscriber conversion.
- Thumbnail overhaul lifted channel CTR from **2.8% → 4.5%+**.
- ~**90% of traffic is Suggested/Browse** (algorithm-driven), not search. Search is a
  minor, under-exploited lane worth some SEO.
- **First breakout = the "German Snipers / what came next" video** (hit the Browse/home
  feed). Lesson: when a topic pops, note it, don't touch the video, make more like it.
- **Watch-hours accelerate hard via long-form** (each dossier view banks ~0.4–0.6 hrs);
  **subscribers are the bottleneck gate.**
- **RPM realistic band: $8–12** (history is a mid-tier RPM category; length + retention
  get you to the TOP of the band, not into finance/health territory). Not $20.
