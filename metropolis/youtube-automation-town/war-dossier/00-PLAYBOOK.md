- **Angle that's working right now:** **WW2 from the German soldier's perspective on Americans** (the WW2 Dossier formula). It's curiosity-driven, evergreen, and has a near-infinite title template.
- **Avoid:** pure current-news angles (e.g. Frontline's Ukraine coverage earns $20k/mo but dies the day the war ends). Stay evergreen.

## 2. CHANNEL BRANDING

### Recommended name: **The War Dossier**
- **Handle:** `@TheWarDossier` (check availability; fallbacks `@WarDossierHQ`, `@TheWarDossier_`)
- **Why:** "Dossier" signals *declassified files / secret records* → instant curiosity. Authoritative, evergreen, not tied to one war. Sits right next to the proven "WW2 Dossier" and "War Documents" names without copying them.
- **Tagline:** *"The stories the history books left out."*

### Alternate names (all niche-validated)
1. **Frontline Dossier** — punchy, combines two proven names
2. **The Iron Record** — distinctive, evocative
3. **Echoes of War** — emotional, broad
4. **Theatre of War** — classic, premium feel
5. **The Last Dispatch** — strong, memorable

### Visual identity
- **Logo:** a worn manila **"CLASSIFIED" file folder** with a red stamp, or an eagle/iron-cross-neutral military insignia in **aged gold on charcoal**. Keep it simple — readable at 24px.
- **Palette:** charcoal black `#1a1a1a`, aged gold `#c5a253`, blood red `#8b1a1a`, desaturated khaki `#7a7256`. Sepia/desaturated — *never* bright. This signals "serious archival history."
- **Font:** a stencil or slab military serif (e.g. *Stardos Stencil*, *Oswald*, *Special Elite* typewriter) for thumbnails.

### Thumbnail formula (this is 50% of the click)
- **One** dramatic sepia/colourised archival photo (a soldier's face, a tank, an explosion).
- **3–5 huge words** in cream/gold stencil, high contrast: e.g. **"WHY GERMANS FEARED THIS"**.
- A red circle or arrow on the key detail.
- A faint "TOP SECRET" / "DECLASSIFIED" stamp in the corner for the file motif.
- Faces + emotion outperform hardware. Always show a human if you can.

### Channel description (paste into YouTube)
> The War Dossier opens the files history tried to forget. Every week we dig into the
> untold moments of World War II and beyond — the decisions, the soldiers, and the
> secrets that shaped the 20th century. Real history, told like it mattered. New
> dossiers every week. 🎖️
