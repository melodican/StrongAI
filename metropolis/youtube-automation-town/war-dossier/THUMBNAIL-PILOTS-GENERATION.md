# Pilots Thumbnail — Generation Spec
*For "Why German Pilots Refused to Engage American Bombers" — your #1 video,
weakest packaging. Goal: replace generic aircraft + low-contrast "SUICIDE MISSION"
with a high-tension, high-contrast image that lifts CTR off the flat 3.0%.*

---

## STEP 1 — Generate the IMAGE only (no text — AI renders text as garbage)

**Primary prompt (lone fighter vs. the wall of bombers):**

> Cinematic World War II aerial scene, a single small German Messerschmitt fighter
> plane in the foreground lower-left, dwarfed by an enormous overwhelming formation
> of dozens of American B-17 heavy bombers filling the entire sky behind it,
> dramatic scale contrast, the lone fighter looking outnumbered and turning away,
> shafts of cold sunlight through clouds, desaturated sepia-and-steel colour grade,
> high contrast, gritty film grain, ominous and tense atmosphere, archival
> documentary look, photorealistic, 16:9, leave clear darker space in the upper-right
> third for a text overlay.

**Alternative prompt (pilot's face — faces beat hardware):**

> Extreme close-up of a young German WWII fighter pilot's face inside the cockpit,
> leather flight helmet and goggles pushed up, eyes wide with fear and dread looking
> out at the sky, reflection of a massive bomber formation in the cockpit glass,
> desaturated sepia-and-steel colour grade, dramatic side lighting, high contrast,
> gritty film grain, archival documentary realism, photorealistic, 16:9, leave clear
> space on the right third for a text overlay.

> **Generate BOTH and pick the stronger.** The face version usually wins on CTR
> (emotion + a human), but the scale-contrast version tells the story instantly.
> A/B them if you can.

---

## STEP 2 — Overlay text + arrow (in Canva / Photoshop / VidRush graphics)

- **Text:** `THEY TURNED AWAY` — cream or white stencil/slab font (Oswald, Stardos
  Stencil, Special Elite), in or beside a **blood-red box** for max contrast.
  - Shorter alt: `TOO MANY` or `THEY REFUSED`
- **Placement:** upper-right third (where the prompt left clear space). 2–3 words, huge.
- **One white arrow** from the lone fighter pointing to the bomber stream (scale
  version), or pointing at what the pilot is staring at (face version).
- **Optional:** faint `DECLASSIFIED` stamp, bottom-left corner, ~30% opacity — the
  dossier motif that ties your catalogue together.

---

## STEP 3 — The 3-second test
Shrink it to phone-thumbnail size. Can you (a) read the words and (b) feel the
tension in under 3 seconds? If yes, ship it. If the words blur or the image reads as
"just planes," raise contrast / enlarge the text / simplify.

---

## Why this is the right first fix
Pilots has your biggest impression pool (10.7K channel impressions, this video is #1
in distribution) but historically your lowest CTR. Swapping the thumbnail does NOT
reset the video — YouTube keeps it and tests the new thumbnail against the same
audience it's already pushing. So every CTR point gained here is multiplied by the
traffic the algorithm is *already* sending. Highest-leverage single asset on the channel.
