## 3. IMPORTANT — hashtag rules (don't trip YouTube's filter)

- YouTube shows the **first 3 hashtags** in the description ABOVE the video title.
  That's why the footer leads with `#ww2 #worldwar2 #militaryhistory`.
- **Hard rule: 15 hashtags max across the WHOLE description.** Use more than 15 and
  YouTube IGNORES ALL of them. So:
  - **Remove the hashtags from your individual video blurbs** and let this footer's
    three be the consistent default — OR keep a couple of video-specific ones in the
    blurb, but keep the grand total ≤ 8 to be safe. **Do not double up.**

## 4. Other upload defaults worth setting once (same Settings page)
- **Category:** Education (or People & Blogs). Education can help RPM/topic signals.
- **Comments:** On, with "hold potentially inappropriate for review" — keeps the
  comment section clean on a history channel that can attract bad-faith posts.
- **License:** Standard YouTube License.
- **"Altered/synthetic content" disclosure:** if you use AI narration/imagery, tick
  the disclosure where prompted — it's a YouTube requirement now and protects the
  channel. (This is a checkbox at upload, not part of the description.)
- **End screen / branding watermark:** set a default subscribe watermark so it's on
  every video automatically (ties into the subscribe-conversion fix).
