# The War Dossier — Thumbnail Audit (Week 1)
*Fixing the other bottleneck: CTR stuck at 3.0% despite impressions going 2K → 10.7K.
The algorithm is escalating distribution; flat CTR means we're leaving views on the
table. At 5% CTR the same impressions yield ~70% more views — for zero extra spend.*

---

## The standard (set by the "GREEN HELL" Jungle thumbnail — your best)

Why that one works, and what every thumbnail must hit:
1. **ONE desaturated dramatic image** — no clutter.
2. **2–3 HUGE high-contrast words** in a coloured box (red box / cream stencil).
3. **ONE white arrow** pointing to the key detail (directs the eye).
4. **Topically unmistakable at a glance** + readable at phone-thumbnail size.
5. **A human / implied human + imminent danger** beats hardware alone (faces > tanks).
6. **A faint "DECLASSIFIED / TOP SECRET" stamp** in a corner for the dossier motif (optional but on-brand).

**The 3-second test:** glance at it for 3 seconds at small size. Can you read the
words AND feel the tension? If not, it fails.

## Audit of live thumbnails (priority order = current impressions)

### 1. Pilots — "Why German Pilots Refused to Engage American Bombers" (140 views, your #1)
- **Current:** aircraft image, "SUICIDE MISSION" text (dark/blue).
- **Problem:** this is your most-distributed video but historically your *lowest CTR*
  (1.96% early). The text colour is low-contrast and the image reads as generic
  aircraft — no single focal point, no human, no arrow. It's winning on topic, not packaging.
- **FIX (highest priority — most impressions ride on this):**
  - Image: a lone German fighter pilot's face (goggles/fear) OR a single fighter
    dwarfed against a massive wall of B-17 bombers filling the sky.
  - Text: **"THEY TURNED AWAY"** or **"TOO MANY"** in cream/red high-contrast stencil.
  - White arrow from the lone fighter to the bomber stream.
  - This is the one to fix first — every CTR point here is amplified by your biggest impression pool.

### 2. Rations — "Why German Soldiers CRAVED American Rations" (94 views)
- **Current:** "GERMANS COVETED" red text, hand with rations, white arrow. **Closest to standard already.**
- **Problem:** minor — "COVETED" is a weaker, more abstract word than "CRAVED" (which is in your title and is more visceral). Image is good.
- **FIX (light touch):** change text to **"THEY CRAVED THIS"** to match the title's
  punch; keep the hand + arrow. Add the DECLASSIFIED stamp. Otherwise leave it — it works.

### 3. Artillery — "Why German Soldiers Feared American Artillery" (86 views)
- **Current:** explosion/artillery fire with a "00:00:00" timer graphic.
- **Problem:** the timer graphic is a distraction that adds no meaning — it dilutes the
  focal point. No human, no arrow. Strong image, weak focus.
- **FIX:**
  - Image: keep the explosion but add a **terrified soldier's face** in the foreground
    reacting to it (human + danger).
  - Text: **"THE SILENT KILLER"** or **"NO WARNING"** (ties to the script's "death from
    a silent sky" theme) in red box.
  - Drop the timer. Add a white arrow to the incoming shell/explosion.

### 4. POWs — "What Stunned German POWs About American Field Hospitals" (70 views)
- **Current:** "SAVED BY THE ENEMY" / "AMERICAN FIELD HOSPITALS" — **two text blocks = too much.**
- **Problem:** two competing text lines shrink each other and break the "3-second read."
  Faces are likely present (good) but text is doing too much.
- **FIX:** cut to ONE line — **"SAVED BY THE ENEMY"** (it's the stronger, more
  curiosity-driving phrase). Let the title carry "field hospitals." One wounded
  soldier's face + a medic. Arrow to the medic's hands.

### 5. Snipers — "What German Snipers Noticed About American Infantry" (35 views)
- **Current:** dark ruins scene — **too dark, no clear subject, no text legible at size.**
- **Problem:** lowest performer and it shows. The image is murky and reads as nothing
  at phone size. No human focal point.
- **FIX:**
  - Image: a sniper's eye through a scope, OR a crosshair framed on an American soldier.
  - Text: **"THEY ALWAYS KNEW"** or **"ONE TELL"** in high-contrast cream stencil.
  - Brighten/raise contrast hard — dark thumbnails die in the feed. Arrow to the target in the crosshair.

## Cross-cutting fixes (apply to ALL)
- **Faces + emotion in every thumbnail.** Your strongest images all imply a human in
  danger. Hardware-only thumbnails (aircraft, explosions, ruins) underperform.
- **One text block, 2–3 words, max contrast.** Kill any second line.
- **One arrow, always white, always pointing at the payoff detail.**
- **Brightness/contrast pass** — several of these are too dark for the feed.
- **Consistent visual language** = stronger topic-cluster signal to the algorithm
  (which is already feeding you via suggested videos at 90.9%).

## Priority order (do in this sequence)
1. **Pilots** — biggest impression pool, weakest packaging. Maximum leverage.
2. **Snipers** — worst thumbnail, easiest win, low base to climb from.
3. **Artillery** — drop the timer, add a face.
4. **POWs** — cut to one text line.

> Note: re-uploading/swapping a thumbnail on a video that's currently getting
> impressions resets nothing — YouTube keeps the video; it just starts testing the new
> thumbnail against the same audience. So fixing Pilots' thumbnail directly improves
> the video the algorithm is *already* pushing hardest. No downside.
